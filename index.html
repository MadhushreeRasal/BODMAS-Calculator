What is BODMAS?

BODMAS stands for Brackets, Orders, Division, Multiplication, Addition and Subtraction. It tells us the order in which mathematical operations should be performed to solve an expression correctly.

B

BRACKETS

O

ORDERS

D

M

A

ADDITIONS

S

SUBSTRACTIONS

This is a simplified BODMAS calculator designed to handle basic arithmetic expressions involving addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. Enter your expression, press =, and let the calculator handle the order of operations for you!
